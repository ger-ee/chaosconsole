Add ChaosConsole to your Mac, iPhone, and iPad. The same rooms, in their own app window, with a place on your Dock or Home Screen.

Open your rooms →

01 / DESKTOP

On your Mac

  1. Open chaosconsole.com in Safari.
  2. Choose File → Add to Dock, or use the Share menu.
  3. Name it ChaosConsole, then click Add.
02 / PHONE

On your iPhone

  1. Open chaosconsole.com in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen. You may need to tap More first.
  3. Keep Open as Web App on if shown. Name it ChaosConsole and tap Add.
03 / TABLET

On your iPad

  1. Open chaosconsole.com in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen. You may need to tap More first.
  3. Keep Open as Web App on if shown. Name it ChaosConsole and tap Add.

Install once on each device. Rooms need an internet connection; published site updates appear in the app. Your unlock session and locally saved preferences stay with each device. On Mac, Add to Dock requires macOS Sonoma or later.
