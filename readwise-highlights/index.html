Where themes meet

Passages that carry two ideas at once. These are the seams of the library — where one subject stops being separate from another.

How this was built. — Readwise highlights were pulled from the library on —; onboarding boilerplate and an idiom reference were removed and duplicate passages collapsed, leaving — passages of real reading. Each was scored against a weighted lexicon for sixteen themes — strong terms counted triple, supporting terms singly — and assigned to as many as three whose score cleared the bar. — passages matched at least one theme; — did not, which is the honest result for narrative and memoir that carries no strong conceptual vocabulary. Node size is passage count; a link means two themes co-occur in the same passage at least three times. Classification is lexical, not semantic — it is a map of the library's vocabulary, and a passage that gestures at power without ever naming it will sit outside the cluster it belongs to. The library is re-pulled from Readwise on the first of every month and the map rebuilt from it; the newest highlight in this build is dated —.
