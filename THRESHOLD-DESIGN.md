# Threshold / Chaos Console

The approved Threshold landing concept and its Ledger study are the visual authority for this rollout. This is a presentation and navigation update, not a source-data refresh.

## System

- Oxblood `#3B1720` is the structural ink; ivory `#FFF1DD` is the working surface.
- Terracotta `#A74729` is the primary accent, apricot `#F3BE83` the light accent.
- Plum `#21191F` supports immersive maps; rose-brown `#795E5D` carries secondary text.
- Barlow Condensed supplies architectural titles and large figures. Barlow carries interface text; Source Serif 4 supplies reading headings and quotations.
- The existing Threshold architectural artwork is reused, with crops and restrained room-family color treatments. It is not replaced with a generic stock image.

```
All rooms:     [Quiet brand / parent room / room switcher]

               [Large, uninterrupted working canvas]
```

The system must not flatten every room into the same card grid. Existing quotations, provenance, uncertainty, canonical color meanings, calculations, controls, and data pipelines remain intact. Technical captions that describe the old presentation can be removed; substantive source material stays available. Longer operational narratives become disclosures. Important caveats remain next to the figures they qualify.

## Boundaries

- Preserve the client-side gate and existing source data.
- Keep Hearing Room logistics-only; do not add legal interpretation or update its dated record.
- Preserve Ledger statement-month rules and every estimate/carry-forward caveat.
- Binder content is not being ingested or rewritten; this is its visual presentation only.
- Keep all redirects and deep links working.
- Review desktop, tablet, and mobile, including charts, navigation, search, drawers, keyboard controls, and long-form reading.
- No publication until instructed to make it live.
