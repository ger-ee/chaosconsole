Rules for the room

earned in August · they apply on the 28th
Everything goes through counsel.Opposing counsel already tried you directly once. If it happens again: "I'm represented — please contact Anthony Lai at The Zhou Law Group," then forward the voicemail, text or email to him within the hour, Yaling copied. Nothing else.
Zero contact with the petitioner or any protected person.In either direction, through anyone. The order is in force until Oct 28 at least.
Answer, then stop.One sentence. If they want more they'll ask. The clause after the comma is where cases are lost.
Never characterise your own intent."I was worried about rent" is a fact. "I never meant it as a threat" is an argument, and it invites the follow-up that hurts.
Never say their word for them.Make them say it. Silence is not yours to fix — count to three.
Ask before you explain.Lead with the question; add context only if it's wanted. The best analysis of the whole sprint came from one plain question asked late.
Budget for the moot.Two of them — direct, then hostile cross. It is the line item that matters most for you specifically. Do not let it be the one cut for cost.
Never accept a verbal anything.Fees, dates, positions: written, or it didn't happen. The 8/13 conflict-check letter and the 8/19 "continued to October 28" email are the model.
If you lose your place on the phone with the firm: "I'm the respondent, the hearing is October twenty-eighth, and I need [the conformed order / the readout / the onboarding list] in writing this week." Then stop, and let them ask.
