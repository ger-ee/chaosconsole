Why the money sits still. If the court finds the unit was not habitable, it sets a reduced rent for the period and gives you five days to pay it and keep possession. Money that is not liquid on that fifth day is a lost apartment. So $5,400 stays where it can move the same afternoon.

October is different. It is tendered, in full, in a form they can bank, with a letter that says what it is and what it is not. Whether they take it or refuse it, the record shows a tenant paying current rent and disputing two months.

Rules for the room

carried over from the other courtroom · they apply here
Written, or it did not happen.Every call to the owner's counsel or the city gets a same-day email that says what was said. Dates, amounts, names.
Dates, not adjectives."Open since August 6" beats "forever". "Nine tickets since May 2024" beats "constantly". The portal did the counting; quote it.
Answer, then stop.One sentence. If they want more they will ask. The clause after the comma is where cases are lost.
Never characterise anyone's intent."The ticket was marked Emergency on Aug 6 and is still assigned to the vendor" is a fact. "They never cared" is an argument, and it invites the follow-up that hurts.
Three of everything.Court, the other side, you. Tabbed, in the order of the exhibit index. A document you cannot hand over does not exist.
Read the writ clause.Nothing gets signed in a hallway that ends possession automatically if a date is missed. Read to the end before the pen moves; ask for a minute if you need it.
Arrive at 7:45.Stanley Mosk, 111 North Hill Street. Parking and security are both worse than you expect; the Self-Help line forms before the doors open.
Pay current rent in the open.October goes in on time, in full, by cashier's check, with a letter. Refusal is their choice and their record.
If you lose your place at the window: "I'm the defendant in an unlawful detainer, I'm filing my Answer today, and I need conformed copies." Then stop, and let them ask.
