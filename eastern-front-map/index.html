A note on the corpus

The library is strongest on 1944–45 and the aftermath (Beevor, Lowe, Ullrich, Huber, Merridale) — which is exactly the series' lane. It is thin on Stalingrad, the 1942 rage machine's primary texts (Ehrenburg beyond one quote), and the filtration system — those beats will need sourcing beyond current highlights. Kershaw and Kotkin material is prewar context: footnote-level for this series.
